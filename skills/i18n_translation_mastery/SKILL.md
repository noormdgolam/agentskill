# i18n Translation Mastery Skill

## Core Principles
1. **Unbroken DOM Translation**: Perform exact full text-node matching first. Restrict partial regex replacements strictly to long phrases (length >= 25 characters) to prevent sub-word fragmentation.
2. **Dynamic Typography**: Swap font stacks cleanly when switching `html[lang="bn"]` or `html[lang="en"]` without distorting container layouts.
3. **Natural Grammar Standards**: Follow pro-level grammatical guidelines (প্রমিত ও মার্জিত চলিত বাংলা) with natural verb conjugations and polite phrasing.
